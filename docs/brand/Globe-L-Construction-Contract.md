# Publisher Guard Globe-L Construction Contract

## Purpose

The mark is a **calm local-control globe**, not a literal 3D planet, folder, badge, or generated illustration. Its job is to make the `L` identifiable at executable-icon size while the globe only supplies context and depth.

## Fixed Construction Rules

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Grid | Build on a 64-unit square construction grid; preserve a 6-unit transparent perimeter. |
| Globe | Use one outer circumference, two restrained meridians, and at most two latitude arcs. Avoid dense wireframes. |
| L | Keep the L centered, upright, and visibly separate from globe lines. Its vertical stem and foot need a clear 2:1 visual weight ratio. |
| Color | Dark graphite orb, desaturated cool-blue lines, one off-white/ice L. No lime, violet NFT lighting, amber delivery cue, gradient shine, or 3D bevel. |
| Small size | At 24px, the L must read before any globe grid detail. Reduce or omit secondary arcs in the ICO raster. |
| Background | True alpha outside the disc. No app card, square matte, checkerboard, cast shadow, text, or wordmark. |

## Rejection Tests

Reject the prototype if it looks like a stock globe, a generic travel icon, a 3D game asset, a file/folder, a checkmark badge, or if the `L` visually turns into a checkmark or merges into the longitude line.

## Review Gate

The final SVG/PNG/ICO is **not integrated** into Splash, title chrome, or executable packaging until the small mark is reviewed on a dark background at 24px, 40px, and 96px.
